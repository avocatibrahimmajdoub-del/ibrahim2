Your questions

Frequently asked questions

Which area matches my problem?

Describe your situation through the form or by phone: the firm assesses the facts and points you to the right area, even if your case spans several.

How are fees set?

Agreed in writing from the start: flat fee, hourly rate or a success fee within permitted limits. An estimate is given before any work.

Does the firm work outside Sousse?

Yes: the firm pleads before the courts of Sousse and acts across Tunisia, and remotely for clients living abroad.
