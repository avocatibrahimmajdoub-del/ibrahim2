Our work

What we handle

01

Formal notice

Lawyer's letter, negotiation, secured payment plan: obtaining payment without trial where possible.

02

Payment order

Fast ex parte procedure for undisputed claims: obtaining an enforceable title at lower cost.

03

Garnishment

Freezing sums held by a third party (bank, client, tenant): the most effective route against solvent debtors.

04

Seizure & sale

Seizure of movable assets, auction, distribution of proceeds: enforcement through a bailiff.

Our method

How a case unfolds

01

Claim audit

Amount, maturity, evidence, debtor's solvency: we verify the claim is recoverable before incurring costs.

02

Amicable phase

Formal notice and negotiation: a significant share of files settle at this stage, without trial.

03

Enforceable title

Payment order or action on the merits: we obtain the title that allows forced enforcement.

04

Forced enforcement

Garnishment, seizure, property seizure: we direct the bailiff and follow every step until payment.

Good to know

A judgment won is only worth what is enforced. Garnishment in the hands of a bank or third party is often the fastest weapon — provided the attachable sums are identified at the right moment.

Your questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I recover an unpaid invoice in Tunisia?

Formal notice first, then a payment order if the claim is undisputed, then enforcement (garnishment, seizure). The firm chooses the fastest route according to the debtor.

What is garnishment?

A procedure that freezes, in the hands of a third party (bank, employer, client), the sums owed to the debtor, to allocate them to the creditor. It is one of the most effective enforcement routes.

Can I recover without going to court?

Often yes: a lawyer's formal notice and a negotiated, secured payment plan settle many files. Litigation is only started if necessary.

What are the fees for debt collection?

Agreed in writing: a flat fee for the amicable phase, then a regulated success fee on sums actually recovered, within the limits permitted by professional rules.
