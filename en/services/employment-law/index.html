Good to know

In Tunisia, a dismissal without a real and serious cause, or without following the required procedure, gives rise to compensation. Time limits to act are short — notably before the labour court: do not delay consulting.

Your questions

Frequently asked questions

What is unfair dismissal in Tunisia?

A dismissal without a real and serious cause, or without following the statutory or contractual procedure. The judge assesses the damage based on seniority, age, family situation and circumstances.

What is the deadline to challenge a dismissal?

Claims must be brought before the labour court within one year. After that, the right to act may be lost: consult quickly.

Can employers also consult?

Yes: the firm advises employers on disciplinary procedure, contract termination and dispute prevention, with the same rigour it defends employees.

What are the fees for an employment case?

Agreed in writing from the start: flat fee, hourly rate or a success fee within permitted limits. An estimate is given before any work.
